Wage optimisation — as language, not advice

How take-home pay is commonly structured in Australia. Sit with a registered tax agent before acting.

What the words mean

Salary packaging

A written deal with the employer to send part of gross pay to super before it is taxed at marginal rates.

Concessional vs non-concessional

Concessional amounts are taxed in the fund, usually 15% for most members. Non-concessional amounts are after-tax.

Offsets

This sketch ignores offsets so nobody treats the number as a refund estimate.

Independence

Each adult keeps their own super member account. A community levy is an after-tax outgoing.
