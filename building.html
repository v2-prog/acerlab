Building-code basics — what to ask

Class 1a

Typical detached house. A second dwelling or common kitchen can push the job out of this box.

Class 2

Usual language for apartments. Fire separation, egress and often accessibility step up.

Moveable dwellings

A tiny house on wheels may be a caravan, a dwelling, or neither. Get the use definition in writing.

Common house

A building used by more than one household can pick up different fire and access duties.
