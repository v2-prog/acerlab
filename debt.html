Debt literacy

Minimum payments keep the account open; they are rarely the cheapest path off the balance.

Good debt / bad debt — a teaching frame

Working debt

A loan attached to an asset that can be sold, on a rate you can service from ordinary income.

Draining debt

High-rate revolving balances funding things that fall in value.

Shared-living trap

A joint mortgage is simple for the bank. A co-ownership deed does not bind the lender.

Independence

Do not guarantee another household’s dwelling loan. Cap any guarantee in writing.
